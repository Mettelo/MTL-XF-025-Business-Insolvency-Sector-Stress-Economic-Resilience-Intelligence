# MTL-XF-025 — Business context

## Organisation and decision owner

**Simulated client:** Confidential Economic Development & Business Resilience Directorate. **Accountable executive:** Director of Economic Development & Business Resilience. **Review cadence:** monthly business resilience review.

This fictional directorate/board is an engagement scenario, not an assertion of a real client's circumstances. The source data remains official public/open data.

## Business problem

The economic-development team must decide where additional business-support intelligence is needed when insolvency counts move sharply. A spike may reflect connected companies entering a procedure rather than a broad rise in business failure. National monthly totals, event records and registered company locations also do not necessarily describe the same population or trading footprint.

## Why the current process is insufficient

The current scenario relies on separately prepared reports without a common source/metric contract. The material failure to prevent is specific: Connected-company events and incomplete coding may be mistaken for broad local economic distress.

This creates uncertainty over which cases need investigation and which comparisons should be rejected. No quantified loss, backlog, budget deficit or service failure is invented as a client fact.

## Commissioned decision

Which sectors or recorded geographic concentrations of company insolvency warrant business-support investigation, after distinguishing count growth, coverage and insolvency procedures?

## Practical management use case

At the monthly review, the Director sees the procedure driving a count increase, checks whether the movement persists across three- and six-month windows, then reviews available sector coding. If a cluster appears linked to one corporate group or has poor geographic coverage, the team requests corroborating evidence before targeting an area-wide support response.

## Current-to-target workflow

1. **Before review:** analyst registers the approved files, coverage and definitions; steward checks the cohort and unresolved exceptions.
2. **At review:** Director of Economic Development & Business Resilience opens the overview, then inspects diagnostics and the underlying eligible population before considering a case.
3. **Challenge:** the relevant stakeholder compares the alternative scenario and asks whether the signal survives missingness, comparability and denominator checks.
4. **Action:** each accepted case records investigation type, responsible service role, missing evidence, next review and what finding would reverse the recommendation.
5. **Follow-up:** the next monthly business resilience review records evidence received and the decision, without claiming the analytical signal itself caused an improvement.

## Required analytical response

- Produce procedure/jurisdiction trends separating seasonal adjustment bases and registration dates.
- Analyse industry and geography only where the record-level fields support them; quantify unmatched and uncoded records.
- Shortlist up to ten sector/geographic investigation cases with three-/six-month sensitivity and explicit denominator gaps.

## Measurable project outcomes

1. Every trend identifies jurisdiction, procedure, date basis and seasonal-adjustment status.
2. Every sector/geographic finding reports uncoded/unmapped counts and the actual event-history window.
3. Every early-warning suggestion is labelled unvalidated unless a separate time-based backtest and denominator support it.

## Proposed benefit monitoring

At kickoff, record a baseline review process and propose measures for: time to regenerate the agreed evidence pack; proportion of agenda recommendations with a complete evidence chain; and proportion of accepted investigations with a role owner and next review. The target for recommendation traceability and ownership is 100%; regeneration time is measured during the independent rerun, with no fabricated pre-project baseline or claimed reduction. These are proposed management measures, not achieved service benefits.

## Scope and evidence limits

**Core:** June 2026 long-run CSV plus the agreed England/Wales/Scotland record ZIP and long-run metadata. Establish actual record-level dates, fields and event keys before sector/geographic analysis. National history and observed event history remain separate; all records with missing industry/geography stay visible in coverage diagnostics.

**Extensions:** Risk per active business, trading-site exposure, predictive early warning and causal support effectiveness require additional denominators/evaluation. If the ZIP lacks a required breakdown, use a registered official industry table for sector analysis; do not infer it from company names.

- Counts do not measure insolvency risk per business without a compatible business-population denominator.
- Public company records must not become personal profiles; omit unnecessary names and addresses from outputs.
- Retrospective insolvency indicators are not validated predictive early warnings; forecasting is optional and requires time-based evaluation.

## Risks, mitigations and escalation

- **Interpretation risk:** Connected-company events and incomplete coding may be mistaken for broad local economic distress. The domain specialist reviews definitions and DT1–DT10 before sign-off.
- **Source risk:** Release resources and metadata link verified; ZIP members, event coverage, sector/location schema and frozen checksums pending. The steward records unresolved items at G1; mandatory gaps block dependent outputs.
- **Decision risk:** prioritisation assumptions may affect which cases appear. Show alternative scenarios, excluded cases and evidence that could change a recommendation.
- **Delivery risk:** limited time or skills can leave undocumented manual steps. Assign a second-member rerun and retain contribution/review evidence throughout the eight weeks.

## Evidence chain

Stakeholder requirement → registered source and valid cohort → governed field/metric → tested transformation → reconciled finding → scenario reason code → investigation owner/evidence request → simulated UAT and sign-off.

Every major recommendation must be traceable through this chain.
